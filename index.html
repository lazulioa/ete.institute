[ 01 // CRITICAL INTERFACE ]

Decentralized System Audit

We operate as an independent civil auditing platform. By mapping information-tracking networks, defense mechanisms, and corporate data pipelines into a decentralized system mesh, we visualize the points of friction between militarized data collection and civil society agency.
