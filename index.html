Cultural resolution

A quieter view. Inspired by how human bodies were tokenised and fetishised during the Iranian war, by the state, and by the footage that documents them, it asks what we really archive when a data centre's content is human.

Core values & compositional logic

The Intelligent Twin

A digital twin mirrors a system. An intelligent twin interprets one, fusing quantitative telemetry with qualitative, human testimony so that culture, mourning and civilian experience are tracked parameters, not rounding errors.

Formally: for a set of cases cᵢ, ETE assigns each a weight wᵢ (jurisdictional scale), a deviation vector δᵢ (offset from a normative baseline across several governance axes), a documentation density nᵢ (independent monitoring), and a source-provenance class. A continuous deviation field is reconstructed by inverse-distance-weighted interpolation, h(x) = Σ wₖ(x)·yₖ / Σ wₖ(x) with wₖ(x) = 1/(‖x−pₖ‖² + ε), yielding the topology you navigate. Position is derived from the data, never authored, the method is reproducible and source-traceable end to end. This is civil auditing: an inspectable, contestable evidence layer rather than an opaque score.

How to read the geometry

Every node is placed, not scattered. Height is deviation from a normative baseline, mass and radius are jurisdictional weight, the line-fan is the density of independent documentation, and horizontal distance encodes relationship. Distance is not fixed: switch on Relate and the geometries re-cluster live, so cases that move together on the chosen metric draw together and the slope between them re-forms. The terrain slope is the chart.

The neutrality principle, "co-occurrence offset"

Position is derived, never authored. A case sits where the weight of independent sources puts it, offset from a median baseline by the agreement of many observers. It is the architecture, not ETE, that asserts where a case stands. Neutrality is an emergent property of breadth.

Expanded Human Intelligence (EHI), not AGI

We widen human intelligence into the dataset. A dedicated civilian-impact axis keeps people inside a conflict in frame, and we mine open footage for what watchdog dashboards ignore, rituals of mourning, the cultural texture of harm, the body refused dignity. Evidence of what binary metrics fetishise and overlook, not ornament.

State disclosures and platforms' own transparency data stay visibly distinct from think-tank analysis (IISS, ISDP, MERICS, Chatham House), peer-reviewed work, and ETE's footage-mining layer. When a state and a watchdog disagree, the geometry shows the gap rather than resolving it for you.

The vertical linkage and multidimensional intelligence

The instrument resolves into a multidimensional intelligence: each geometry is an agent that handles one domain of complexity, and agents are stacked across a vertical linkage of Space (orbit, spectrum, satellite constellations, ITU standards), Sky (low-altitude economy, drones, dual-use airspace, mesh networks) and Ground (cables, deep-packet inspection, civilian infrastructure, mourning and memory). Information flows between agents as lines, so a dependency that looks compliant in isolation, like a satellite kill-switch shaping a drone corridor that rests on a fibre landing, becomes legible once the layers are read together. Financial flows, including on-chain blockchain settlement, enter the same surface as agents in their own right.

From orbit to the body, signal fusion & the wearable

The linkage runs further than the ground in both directions. Above it, open space data, telemetry, spectrum, synthetic-aperture radar, is read as an orbital incident registry that flags a space anomaly or a dual-use hop the moment it appears. Below it, the linkage reaches the person: a wearable that receives satellite signals directly and renders them on the body, making the human the final, sovereign node in the chain rather than a passive endpoint. We design that device as part of the system, the same geometry-as-key privacy logic, now worn.

The two registers fuse. The orbital registry and ETE's terrestrial critical-terrain simulation, the wind-tunnel, meet in a single civil-society common operating picture: space anomalies and ground stress read on one surface. That picture translates outward two ways, public-interest insight for human-rights NGOs and investigative journalists, and source-traceable, admissible evidence for international courts and trade-compliance bodies. The body is not outside this system; it is where the signal is finally felt.

A collective governance model

ETE is not a single authored verdict but a collective governance model: the baseline is the median of many independent observers, and a case's position is the emergent product of their agreement. The architecture, not the institute, asserts where a case stands, which is what keeps the reading neutral and contestable. Governance here is a practice of composition, audited in public, rather than a score handed down.

Decentralized physicality, the LoRa plan

ETE need not live in a browser tab. It runs as a decentralized network of local compute: small edge units, meshed over LoRa, each holding part of the landscape and sensing OSINT like a radio tuning a band, off-grid. Privacy is structural: the meaning is legible only under the operator's key, so an uncomposed node leaks nothing. The device is built for this system, not a generic terminal.

How we provide the service

Access is bounded three ways: a gated browser plan; a data-embedding engagement where we model a partner's own evidence; and the dedicated edge device.

Forecast, how world infrastructure shifts

If the method holds, infrastructure is built against mapped dependencies rather than a wishlist, and ETE can advise large-scale builders (for instance, sequencing net-zero technology parks). It interoperates with digital twins but needs none; the model can condense onto a single edge chip. We begin in conflict zones because they are the hardest human problem; if the hardest system can be mapped, the rest can follow.

Beyond auditing, toward structural synthesis

The next stage past auditing is structural synthesis: a live equilibrium infrastructure can route through. A live pulse maps reality as it is; ghost simulations stress-test it, so an operator sees where a corridor calcifies before it breaks. Stable paths fuse into smooth monoliths, fragile ones spike into shards. The geometry becomes the decision surface.

We treat the instrument as a multi-agent system and take its risks seriously. Our reading of cascading failure, phase transitions and emergent collective behaviour is informed by the Cooperative AI Foundation's taxonomy of multi-agent risks (Hammond et al., 2025), which catalogues how network effects and destabilising dynamics turn small perturbations into system-wide shifts. Eternal Terra Ear remains the core; that literature keeps our claims about interdependence and emergence honest rather than rhetorical.

Yes, and that lineage is our credibility. Forensic Architecture pioneered spatial evidence for international courts;1 IISS, ISDP and MERICS publish the conflict and infrastructure datasets we read; Bellingcat, ACLED, OONI and TeleGeography each hold one layer. ETE's new move is a single parametric surface where those layers, legal, infrastructural, financial, cultural, coexist as adjustable agents, cross-examined together.

Terms & privacy

Data handling. The public Atlas runs entirely in your browser; it sets no tracking cookies and sends no telemetry. Trial and commission data you share is processed only to build your landscape, never sold or used to train third-party models, and deleted on request. Source data is attributed, not appropriated: we retain provenance, you retain ownership.

Neutrality & liability. ETE presents derived spatial indices for analysis and advocacy. Indices are interpretive; quoted figures are attributed to their sources. ETE is not a court of fact and makes no determination of state responsibility, provenance tags exist precisely so users can weigh sources themselves.

Fiscal host. ETE is fiscally hosted by Open Source Europe (Brussels).
