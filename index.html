[ 02 // About ]

Eternal Terra Ear

A social enterprise and fluid collective body. Planetary infrastructure platform exploring cultural governance as active curation. Memory not as static archive, but as living system — infrastructural media that sustains itself through crisis.

Mission
By architecturalising relational data into hyperbolic geometry, we reveal the hidden friction in international tech-transfers and infrastructure development. We counter extractivist surveillance with visceral data agency.
Post-Humanitarian Infra
We synthesize fragmented data into sensory narrative. Social architects preserving what mass society neglects — turning the debris of hyperreality into foundational material for a new, conscious social order.
Ritual Data Center
Storing what is most human in the most humanless space. Found footage of funeral, mourning, and burial rites from the global internet — crude, true, lost between the mass of data. The fear of death encapsulated in a dead environment.
Social Enterprise Model
Community-contributable infrastructure. Revenues reinvested into archival operations, legal advisory, and community systems. No extractivist data economy. Every node is attributed. Nothing anonymised without consent.

Add a Node

All contributions are attributed. Nothing is anonymised without consent. Minimum two team members review cultural sensitivity and legal flags before any node is merged into the archive.

03
Fill in: ritual type · region · source URL · cultural context · legal flags · contributor name
04

05
