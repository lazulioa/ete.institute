Rituals of Death, Internet Displacement, and Humanity in a Transcendental Future Creating Collective Memory

We look carefully when referencing technology as "dead machines." Technological systems do not follow the human lifecycle, they exist within a configuration sustained by human energy, cooling systems, and prompts. By embedding found global funeral footage, mourning traditions, and burial rites directly into monumental memory structures, we construct a living testament. This warehouse teaches future biogenetically modeled entities or non-human intelligence how fear and reverence of death defined the mortal species before its code expired.
